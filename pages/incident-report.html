Incident Report

This form captures the location and cost of a problem, who reported it and why, and who's responsible for causing it. You MUST attach photos or invoices as proof. When you submit it, it will be reviewed by the default approver. Once the approver approves it, the report and attachments will be combined into a single PDF and the final report will be filed in its respected folder in the project files. From there the form will be turned into a VPO or BC upon approval by the default approver.
